# 📊 Meesho E-Commerce Business Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This portfolio project analyzes a Meesho e-commerce dataset using Excel, SQL, and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, orders, returns, category performance, geography, sellers, discounts, payment behavior, and products.

The objective was to transform transactional data into actionable business insights and recommendations.

> **Dataset Note:** This is a portfolio case study based on an available e-commerce dataset and does not represent Meesho's internal or confidential business data.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis focuses on:

- Understanding overall sales and order performance
- Identifying high-performing categories, states, sellers, and products
- Measuring and analyzing return performance
- Evaluating discount and payment behavior
- Identifying potential areas for business improvement

---

**Tools:** Excel | SQL | Power BI

**Skills:** Data Cleaning | Data Analysis | DAX | Data Modeling | KPI Development | Data Visualization | Business Analysis

## 🔎 Key Analysis

- Sales and order performance
- Category and product performance
- State and seller performance
- Return rate analysis
- Discount and payment analysis

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **₹94,088** in sales across **50 orders and 102 units**
- Overall **return rate is 30%**, making returns an important area for investigation
- **Women Wear and Men Wear** are the strongest sales-contributing categories
- **Gujarat** records the highest sales among the states analyzed
- **Kids Wear** has the highest observed category return rate at **40%**
- **10–20% discount band** generates the highest sales in the analyzed dataset
- **COD contributes 56% of sales**

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Investigate high-return categories to identify potential product, sizing, quality, or fulfillment issues
- Review high-return regions and sellers using larger datasets
- Evaluate discount effectiveness before increasing discount levels
- Monitor high-performing products for inventory and promotional opportunities
